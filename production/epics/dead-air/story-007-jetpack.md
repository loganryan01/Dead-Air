## Acceptance Criteria

*From the brief's push-your-luck tension (air and weight as the pressures) applied to the user's jetpack idea:*

- [ ] Holding the jump input fires the jetpack and pushes the player upward; on release the player falls under normal gravity.
- [ ] While the jetpack fires it burns extra air per second, on top of the normal load-based drain (air is the fuel).
- [ ] Carried weight reduces thrust; at or above a configured weight the jetpack cannot lift the player off the floor.
- [ ] The station has at least two raised areas reachable only by jetpack, each holding salvage worth more than any ground-level item.
- [ ] The jetpack does nothing once the run has ended (air at zero, collapse, or cash-out).
- [ ] Thrust, extra air cost, weight falloff and maximum lift weight come from a data file.
- [ ] Landing from any height is safe (no fall damage).

---

## Implementation Notes

- Air is the fuel by design: no separate fuel gauge or HUD meter. The existing air meter shows the faster drain.
- Thrust and air-cost math lives in its own script (`jetpack.gd`) so it is testable without the scene.
- Raised areas go in the hand-built station; no procedural placement (the brief rules it out).
- Take a screenshot of the player on a raised area next to its salvage and keep it in `production/qa/evidence/`.
